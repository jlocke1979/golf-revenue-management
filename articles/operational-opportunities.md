# 2. Heatmap: Month and Days

This chapter compares season and weekday on the open days, then records
two receipt changes. It recommends no rate. It has no forecast, no price
elasticity, and no revenue estimate. The weather vignette joins
historical weather to these days. Its elasticity section is a separate
synthetic exercise and leaves these figures unchanged.

Utilization is golfers divided by the city’s daylight estimate. The city
sets that estimate from sunrise to sunset. Golfers per day is the count
of people who played. A day above one hundred percent ran past the
estimate. Tee-sheet occupancy would require a tee time, and this extract
has daily counts.

The rates use the 1,708 days with at least one golfer, from 2021-07-01
through 2026-04-30. A period rate is the sum of golfers divided by the
sum of the daylight estimates, the same ratio as the capacity tables.
Days with no recorded play are omitted. The October zero-round stretches
are in that omission. The city file does not say the course was closed.

## What the comparison shows

Summer’s lower percentage is a longer daylight estimate. June has 275
golfers per open day, against 272 in February. July has 252 and August
has 255. Those months sit above December (231) and January (239), and
below March (303) and April (314). The extract has daily counts, so that
lower percentage is the longer daylight estimate.

Realized green-fee yield is green-fee revenue divided by played rounds.
February and March are about \$46 to \$48 per golfer. June through
August are about \$16 to \$19. The extract has no posted rate and no
rate code in this comparison, so the gap stays a receipt per played
round.

In 2022, 2023, and 2024 the receipt per recorded cart is \$16.00. Carts
per golfer run from 0.78 to 0.80. In 2025 the receipt is \$10.57 and
carts per golfer are 0.42. Total dollars per golfer move from \$99.41 to
\$101.99. The city catalog derives the cart count from cart revenue, so
the 2025 cart series follows that derivation. From January through April
2026, cart dollars per golfer are \$4.09.

Range dollars per golfer are \$11.63 in 2022 and \$27.13 in 2025. The
correlation with daily golfers is 0.20 and 0.09. Both correlations are
weak. The rise in dollars per golfer stands as a receipt change.
