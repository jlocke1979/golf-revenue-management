# golfops: Golf Participation and Revenue Summaries

Summarizes municipal golf participation, daylight-estimated utilization,
and revenue for City of Mesa Dobson Ranch. Operating charts use one
documented daily sample. Revenue ratios are historical relationships,
and tournament results are break-even baselines. Forecasts of observed
rounds are not latent demand and not price elasticities. A bundled NOAA
weather extract supports retrospective playable-capacity scenarios. A
log-log price function reports ordinary least-squares associations, not
course-clustered standard errors, and the synthetic samples are not a
Dobson posted-price elasticity. Labeled synthetic examples cover a
tournament ticket series and one municipal tee sheet.
