# golfops: Golf Participation and Revenue Summaries

Summarizes municipal golf participation, daylight-estimated utilization,
and revenue for City of Mesa Dobson Ranch. Operating charts use one
documented daily sample. Revenue ratios are historical relationships,
and tournament results are break-even baselines. Forecasts of observed
rounds are not latent demand and not price elasticities. Labeled
synthetic examples cover a tournament ticket series and one municipal
tee sheet.
